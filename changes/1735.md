- Grid volumes take `flameTemperature: { ignition, maximum }`, Blender's flame remap (`flame > 0.01 ? ignition + flame * (maximum - ignition) : 0`), so the blackbody comes from the flame channel at 1500 to 3000 K whatever the smoke density, and the add-explosion fireball uses it: the smoke edge glows orange as it does in Cycles. Volumes without the field render as before. The Glaze, CPU, WebGPU and native paths and the fire light share the remap.
