- A web pack now carries an HDRI sky and the texture images of its parts. `environment.hdri` and `keyFromHdri` pack as the decoded sky image (RGBE, at most 1024 pixels wide; a wider file is halved), and `<crucible-scene>` lights the scene with it instead of a named setup. Colour, normal, roughness, metal, occlusion and height maps pack as plain pixels with their colour space, wrap and filter, up to 1,048,576 texels in all, and an image used by several parts is stored once; past the limit pack names the part that holds the most. Studio's web pack export keeps both, and the command line packs the bundled skies and refuses catalog files by name. A scene without a sky image or texture packs to the same bytes as before. (#3004)
