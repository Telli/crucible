- Water: `material.contactFoam { width, hardness, noise, drift, color }` draws a hard white line, a fixed number of screen pixels wide, where blended water (opacity below 1) meets a hull, rock or shore, from the depth of the scene behind it. Off by default. (#2034)
- Water: contact foam skips pixels where the scene behind the water is more than half a metre from the surface (far water, the horizon, sky and shores behind open water), which drew a full-width white band along the far edge of the sea. (#2034)
- Water: `contactFoam.drift` (0 to 1, 0 default) makes the rim tear into segments that slide with time; 0 keeps the static rim. (#2034)
- Water: \`contactFoam.rim\` (true default) set to false draws no contact line, for water that meets hulls and shores with no foam edge; the sea's crest foam stays. (#2034)
