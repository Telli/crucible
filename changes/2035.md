- Ocean: foam is where the surface folds (Jacobian), sized to the wind's whitecap share (Monahan and O'Muircheartaigh 1980), `coverage` (-1 to 1, the foam strength knob) on the `ocean` edit biases it (0 is physical), `foamMemory` (0 by default, opt-in, 8 extra wave evaluations per vertex) makes foam fade behind crests, a water material takes `foamEdge` (`soft` lace or `hard` painted shape) and `foamWidth`, and the docs' open-sea recipe now selects the sheet's edges with `{ "open": true }` so the sea has enough vertices to carry foam (#2035)
- Water: `foamVeins { amount, scale }` on a water material adds a net of thin foam veins that trails out behind the crests, thinning and breaking into patches over the sea instead of stopping on one line; off by default, and with it off the water renders byte for byte as before. (#2035)
- Particles: a groupThreshold surface emitter falls back to the strongest corner when a faint corner leaves no area above the threshold to draw in (#2035)
