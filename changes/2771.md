- A sun bounce or sky trace that stops at a size limit (a CC0 demo scene like Classroom, past the light bake's 200,000 triangles) says why in its report and the log instead of failing silently, `largeScenes: 'proxy'` traces such a scene through an occupancy grid whose values do not depend on the mesh density, and `openScenes: 'proxy'` traces an open scene the same way; both are opt-in, so the default look is unchanged (#2771).
