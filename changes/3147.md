- A material graph node that appears more than once, such as a slope mask gating colour, roughness and bump, is compiled once: it counts once in the node limit and the work budget, and its value is worked out once for each pixel and shared by every output. A scalar beside a colour is also worked out once per pixel instead of once per channel, which halves the bake time of typical snow graphs. Baked maps are byte for byte the same (#3147).
